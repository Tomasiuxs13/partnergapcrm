# Automation and approval gates

Chosen setting: **Approve sends**.

Runs on its own: Ahrefs discovery, sheet writes, dedupe and owner checks, Apollo people search, Apollo email reveals up to 25 credits a day, drafting emails, reply and bounce sync from Gmail.

Waits for approval per batch: sending outreach emails, Slack messages to colleagues, Apollo spend above the daily cap, Everflow writes.

Review batches go out at each client's local morning, using the time zone in Clients column E.
